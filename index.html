A teacher who makes every single lesson feel personal

I am Tanzeel, a dedicated female Online Quran Teacher with 8 years of experience in teaching the Holy Quran to children and adults through online classes. My aim is to provide a comfortable, supportive, and structured learning environment where every student can learn at their own pace with confidence.

I focus on helping students develop accurate Quran reading skills, improve pronunciation and Tajweed, and build a strong understanding of the correct way to recite the Quran. My teaching approach is patient, step-by-step, and tailored to each student's individual learning level and needs.

Whether you are a beginner starting your Quran journey, a child learning basic Quran reading, or an adult looking to improve your recitation and Tajweed, I strive to make every.

  • Ijāzah in Hafs 'an 'Āsim with sanad
  • Trained in tajwīd in Madinah & Cairo
  • 8+ years online teaching experience

Certification & Learning

My teaching method

A simple 4-step structure keeps lessons focused while allowing students to learn at their own pace.

1

Assess

Understand the student's current reading level and learning needs.

2

Teach

Explain the lesson in simple steps and demonstrate correct reading.

3

Practice

Let the student read, repeat and apply the lesson during class.

4

Review

Revise previous work and set a clear focus for the next lesson.
